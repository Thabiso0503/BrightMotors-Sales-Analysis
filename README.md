# 📌 Project Overview

Bright Motors appointed a new **Head of Sales** to improve overall sales performance, optimize inventory management, and expand dealership growth. However, the company lacked clear, data-driven insights into sales performance, customer purchasing behaviour, and regional trends.

This project transforms raw transactional car sales data into meaningful business insights using SQL, enabling stakeholders to make informed strategic decisions.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were to:

- Identify the top-performing vehicle makes and models
- Analyze revenue and profitability trends
- Evaluate regional sales performance
- Understand how pricing and vehicle characteristics influence purchasing behaviour
- Generate recommendations to improve sales and inventory management

# 🔍 Key Insights

### 🚘 Vehicle Performance

- Certain vehicle makes and models consistently generated the highest revenue.
- Premium vehicle categories contributed significantly to overall profitability.

---

### 🌍 Regional Sales Analysis

- Sales performance varied across different regions.
- Some dealerships consistently outperformed others, indicating opportunities for regional expansion.

---

### 💰 Pricing & Customer Behaviour

- Vehicle pricing strongly influenced customer purchasing decisions.
- Lower-mileage vehicles generally attracted higher demand.
- Newer model years showed stronger sales performance than older vehicles.

---

### ⛽ Customer Preferences

- Customer demand differed across fuel types.
- Specific vehicle categories experienced higher sales volumes than others.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

## 🚗 Inventory Management

- Increase inventory levels for high-performing vehicle models.
- Reduce stock of consistently underperforming vehicles.

## 📈 Sales Strategy

- Focus marketing efforts on best-selling vehicle categories.
- Develop targeted promotions for lower-performing regions.

## 💰 Pricing Strategy

- Review pricing models to remain competitive.
- Offer financing options and promotional discounts where appropriate.

## 🌍 Regional Expansion

- Invest in expanding dealerships within high-growth regions.
- Replicate successful sales strategies across lower-performing locations.

## 📢 Marketing Strategy

- Promote the most popular vehicle categories.
- Launch targeted campaigns based on customer preferences and regional demand.
